# Shader references

The circular edge profile and seven-position spectral reconstruction in
`src/window.cpp` are adapted from the AndroidLiquidGlassView-derived shaders in
SoyBeanMilkx/imgui-liquid-glass at commit
`7dfa072d2b69ad57c031f32feaf111148e65116d`:

https://github.com/SoyBeanMilkx/imgui-liquid-glass/blob/7dfa072d2b69ad57c031f32feaf111148e65116d/src/ui/widget/effects/glass/shaders/opengl/liquid_glass.frag

The upstream shader identifies AndroidLiquidGlassView@9cdd915, copyright
(c) 2025 QmDeve, and the MIT license. The Android platform, hooks, input and
widget code are not included in this library.
